\cvsection{About Me}
\begin{cvparagraph}
    I've been writing software for about 10 years, with the last three as a Software Architect on an enterprise insurance platform. Most of my work is backend and API: I own four REST APIs that internal teams and external partners build against, so a lot of my time goes to contracts, versioning, and the design standards that keep them stable as they grow. I care about the things that make an application hold up in production: scalability, reliability, and tests you can actually trust. OO design and the usual design patterns are how I structure a system, I lean on Postgres for data, and I'm comfortable in AWS and Kubernetes along with the CI/CD around them. I like picking up unfamiliar tools, and I like leaving systems more reliable than I found them.
\end{cvparagraph}
