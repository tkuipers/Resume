\cvsection{About Me}
\begin{cvparagraph}
    I've been writing software for about 10 years, and the last three have been mostly distributed systems and APIs as a Software Architect. I build backends that have to stay up: a customer-facing product I architected runs across multiple AWS accounts and has held 99.9\% uptime since launch, and I rebuilt a brittle legacy system into containerized microservices on Kubernetes with a service mesh and real observability behind it. I own four REST APIs that internal teams and external partners depend on, so contracts, versioning, and reliability get a lot of my attention. I work mostly in Python, lean on Postgres, and care about the metrics that tell you whether a system is actually healthy. I like taking a service end to end, from design through the part where it has to not fall over in production.
\end{cvparagraph}
