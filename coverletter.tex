Owning a service end to end, from the design doc through the part where it has to stay up, is how I like to work.

  I lean on design docs to get a team aligned on the hard calls. One I'm proud of is an RFC to move us to trunk-based development with feature flags, so we could decouple deploys from releases and roll changes out gradually. I treat reliability as part of the feature rather than a follow-up: instrument what I ship, set clear bars, and follow through on what production teaches us.

  On the AI side, I use coding assistants and agentic workflows every day, and I've got real opinions on where they speed things up and where they quietly add work. I'd be glad to help a team get good at using them well, with guardrails rather than blind trust.

  Two honest notes. My frontend depth is in TypeScript rather than Angular specifically, and my cloud work has been on AWS rather than Azure. Neither has slowed me down much before. I ramp quickly, and the Azure side isn't far from what I already use, since I've worked with Azure DevOps and Artifacts. I'd rather be straight about that than oversell it.

  I'd be happy to talk more.
