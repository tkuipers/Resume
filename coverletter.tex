I'm applying for the Healthcare Platform Engineer role. Building backend systems that clinicians actually rely on is somewhere I'd very much like to spend the next chapter of my career, and the role description lines up closely with what I've been doing for the last few years: owning distributed backend services end-to-end, designing for scale and reliability, and influencing architecture across teams.

I've spent 10 years in software, with the last 3 focused on architecture and technical leadership at AMA. I design event-driven backends on cloud infrastructure: APIs serving 5 teams with 99.9\% uptime and \$4.3M in annual transactions, serverless workloads handling millions of asynchronous events, and a self-service developer platform that took environment provisioning from 2-3 weeks down to 2 hours. Right now I'm leading the modernization of a legacy document generation system into containerized C\# microservices on Kubernetes, with Datadog for observability across the lot.

The healthcare side matters to me too. I spent a year at Arterys building and operating backend services for a medical imaging AI SaaS used in clinical settings. It's the part of my career I'd most like to get back to: technology where the impact lands on real patient care.

A few honest notes on fit. My day-to-day languages are Python, Java, and TypeScript; I've been hands-on with C\# on the document generation rewrite and earlier in my career, but I wouldn't claim 5 years of daily .NET. My cloud depth is in AWS rather than Azure (Lambda, EKS, SQS, RDS, IAM, Terraform), but the building blocks line up cleanly: Functions, AKS, Service Bus, and CI/CD pipelines all map to work I do regularly. I'd have a real but short ramp on Azure specifics, and I learn quickly.

I lead through technical vision rather than authority: RFCs, code reviews, mentorship, and architectural guidance for a 12-person team, and a seat on AMA's Architecture Steering Committee. I own systems end-to-end, from design through operations.

I'd be happy to talk more about the role and how my background fits.
