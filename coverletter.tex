I also created a helpful website that maps out these environments, allowing our product owner and QA teams to see which pieces of infrastructure are connected to each other (which front-end talks to which back-end, which cards are deployed where, etc).  Increasing the speed and ease of my coworkers' jobs is a really fun endeavor.

  While I don't have any production experience in the Go stack, I have done a side project, hosting game servers on Kubernetes, that heavily uses Go.  As well, I have experience ramping up quickly and love learning new things.

  Alongside all this, I have both concrete management experience, from being a team lead, and soft leadership experience, more recently in my current role through RFCs, mentorship, and code reviews.
