I'm applying for the Staff Full Stack Engineer role at Thinkific. The opportunity to own a technical domain, drive architectural decisions, and mentor engineers while building impactful products aligns with the work I've been doing for the last few years.

I've spent 10 years in software engineering, with the last 3 in technical leadership and architecture. I designed and own event-driven, multi-tenant systems serving 5 teams with 99.9\% uptime and processing +\$4.3M in annual transactions. I built a self-service developer platform that reduced environment provisioning from weeks to 2 hours, adopted by 15 engineers across 3 teams. I also architected AWS infrastructure for a product handling 2.8 million events that won a 2024 Guidewire Innovation Award.

My backend experience is strong: Python, Java, TypeScript, and Node.js with Postgres, Redis, and Elasticsearch. I work extensively with event-driven architectures using AWS (Lambda, SQS, EKS), Terraform for infrastructure as code, and Kubernetes for container orchestration. I'm comfortable with async job processing, domain-driven design, and building multi-tenant systems that scale.

I don't have production React or Next.js experience, which I know is part of the full-stack expectation. My background is heavily backend and infrastructure focused. I can work through frontend code and have used TypeScript, but I'd have a learning curve on the React/component library side of things. I learn quickly and am comfortable picking up new technologies.

I lead through technical vision and mentorship, not authority. I set standards for a 12-person team through RFC authorship, code reviews, and architectural guidance. I'm a member of our Architecture Steering Committee. I own technical domains end-to-end: architecture, operations, and ongoing evolution.

I'd be happy to discuss how my backend and infrastructure experience could contribute to Thinkific's platform, and how I could grow into the frontend aspects of the role.
