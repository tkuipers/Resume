I'm applying for the Staff Full-Stack Engineer role on Elation's AI product team. The combination of clinical impact and AI-driven product work is exactly the kind of thing I'd like to be doing next: building services that improve outcomes for physicians and patients, with room to shape how AI shows up in the product.

I've spent 10 years in software, with the last 3 focused on architecture and technical leadership at AMA. I have a lot fo side project experience with Python.  Alongside that, I own 4 REST APIs that serve 5 internal teams and external partners and process \$4.3M in annual transactions; I architected an event-driven serverless system that has held 99.9\% uptime since launch and processed 2.8 million events; and I built a self-service developer platform that took environment provisioning from 2-3 weeks down to 2 hours. The Python and AWS parts of your stack are home territory for me.

The healthcare space is where I'd most like to land. I spent a year at Arterys building and maintaining backend services that ran medical AI algorithms and returned results to the platform, with caching and infrastructure tuning to bring algorithm processing turnaround down. It's the closest thing to what Elation does that I've worked on, and the part of my career I've been wanting to get back to.

On the AI side, I haven't shipped LLM-powered features in a product yet, but I follow what's happening in the AI space closely and would jump at the chance to build within it.

Unfortunately, I don't have heavy production React experience; my background is backend and infrastructure focused. I can read and work through frontend code, and TypeScript is a frequently used language for me, but I'd have a real ramp on the React side. I learn quickly, and I'm comfortable being someone who picks up unfamiliar territory.

I lead through technical vision rather than authority: RFCs, code reviews, mentorship for a 12-person team, and a seat on AMA's Architecture Steering Committee. I own systems end-to-end, from design through operations and triage.

I'd be happy to talk more about the role and how my background could fit for this role.
