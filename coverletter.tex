I balance technical excellence with pragmatic delivery, and I care deeply about building solutions that create real customer impact.

The teams that interest me most are Subscription Infrastructure and Harbour Foundations. Both focus on backend systems, scalability, and building foundational platforms that others build on top of, which is where my experience and passion align.
