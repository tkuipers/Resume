I'm applying for the Staff Software Engineer position. Creating a single platform for credit and financial data and focusing on the people that the traditional system overlooks is the kind of challenge I would enjoy tackling for years. What you describe in the role, including enterprise-grade APIs, new integrations with data suppliers, and platform architecture that supports multiple products, closely resembles my current work. 

For the past three years, I have been the software architect at AMA. I manage four REST APIs that support five internal teams and external partners, generating \$4.3 million a year. I also establish contracts, versioning, and design standards, and approve new endpoints. A significant portion of my recent work has involved integrations. I created an event-driven pipeline that pulls policy data from an external partner into our platform using S3, a Kubernetes job, and transactional API calls. This aligns closely with the process of launching new integrations with essential data suppliers. 

On the platform side, I designed the serverless system behind Canada’s first pay-as-you-go auto insurance product, which has maintained 99.9\% uptime since its launch and processed 2.8 million trips. I also developed a self-service developer platform that reduced environment provisioning time from two to three weeks down to two hours. I am comfortable working with Python, TypeScript, and Postgres on AWS, so your technology stack is familiar to me. 

Before this, I spent a year at Arterys, a medical imaging AI startup that was later acquired by Tempus. There, I built backend services using Node and TypeScript. That environment was smaller and faster-moving than my current one, reminding me of my preferred work style. 

I lead by providing technical vision instead of relying on a reporting line. I handle RFCs, conduct code reviews, mentor a 12-person team, and serve on AMA's Architecture Steering Committee. I am accustomed to taking ambiguous concepts and transforming them into actionable plans for other teams. I would be glad to discuss the role further and explore how I could contribute.
